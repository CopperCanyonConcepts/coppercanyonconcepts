Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Our approach

Copper Canyon Concepts LLC respects your privacy and collects only the information needed to communicate with you, operate this website, and provide services you request.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the content of your message, and any information you choose to include. Please do not send confidential customer records, credentials, payment information, health information, or other sensitive material through ordinary email.

Website information

This website does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking, analytics scripts, account registration, or a web contact form. Our hosting and network providers may process basic technical information such as internet protocol address, browser type, request time, and requested pages for security, delivery, and reliability.

How we use information

We may use information to respond to an inquiry, evaluate a potential business relationship, provide requested services, protect our systems, comply with law, and maintain business records. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

We may use service providers for website hosting, email, security, business operations, and approved technology integrations. They receive only the information reasonably needed to perform their services and remain subject to their own legal and security obligations.

LinkedIn integration

If Copper Canyon Concepts activates an official LinkedIn integration, the integration will be used only for approved company Page management, publishing, engagement review, and related analytics. LinkedIn account data will be processed according to the permissions granted by the Page administrator and LinkedIn’s applicable terms. We will not use that connection to publish to personal profiles, send direct outreach, or manage advertising unless separately authorized.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, legal obligations, security, and legitimate business records. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete information you previously provided, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. You may also ask us to stop using information for a particular purpose.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when our website, services, or legal obligations change. The effective date above will identify the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact operations@coppercanyonconcepts.com.
