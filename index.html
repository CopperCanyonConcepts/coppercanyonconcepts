How we work

Start small. Prove the handoff. Expand with evidence.

  1. 1

    Map one workflow

    Identify the real task, responsible people, inputs, exceptions, and required approvals.

  2. 2

    Establish a baseline

    Measure delay, rework, missing information, review effort, and the current result.

  3. 3

    Run a bounded pilot

    Test with least privilege, visible logs, stop conditions, and people still making material decisions.

  4. 4

    Operate only when it works

    Expand carefully, preserve auditability, and keep the business in control.

Current focus

Operationally intensive small businesses.

Our current research includes specialty contractors and field service teams managing estimates, project handoffs, documentation, approvals, billing preparation, recurring reporting, and closeout.

We are listening first and building only around problems that can be measured.

The standard

Useful technology should strengthen judgment, not remove it.

Customer data stays customer owned.

Permissions remain narrow and visible.

Material actions require human approval.

Every workflow keeps an audit trail.

Start with the friction

One repeated workflow may be enough to begin.

If a process repeatedly stalls, gets missed, or depends on a spreadsheet and memory, we would be glad to compare workflows.
