Illustrative sample, not client work

Closeout to Invoice Readiness

This example shows how one bounded workflow could make missing records and approval status visible before final invoicing. No savings or performance result is claimed.

  1. 01

    Work marked complete

    An accountable person starts the closeout check.

  2. 02

    Required records checked

    Expected photographs, documents, tests, and signatures are compared with the agreed list.

  3. 03

    Missing items routed

    Each gap is assigned to a named owner instead of remaining in a message thread.

  4. 04

    Human approval recorded

    A designated reviewer confirms completeness and decides whether exceptions are acceptable.

  5. 05

    Invoice ready packet prepared

    Approved records are organized for the existing billing process.

  6. 06

    Exceptions remain visible

    Open items, decisions, and timestamps remain available for review.

What stays human

People define the requirements, accept exceptions, approve completion, and authorize invoicing. Nothing is sent or approved automatically.
